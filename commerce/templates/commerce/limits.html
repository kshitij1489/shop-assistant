Supported commerce limits

One selected checkout location per tenant; one active connection per role/location. POS and payment may use different providers.

Cash or one online payment attempt per order, in the accepted currency. Online fulfillment requires an exact full capture before expiry. No split tenders, gift cards, partial captures, tips or currency conversion. Refund observations use cumulative totals; provider refund execution and individual refund accounting belong to the adapter.

Inventory uses whole-unit item, variant and addon quantities at the selected location. Strict stock requires fresh numeric counts; availability-only and untracked policies cannot guarantee quantity-based overselling prevention. Refunds and POS cancellations do not automatically restock. No weighted quantities or cross-location allocation.

Fresh stock does not reserve inventory against independent POS sales. A global guarantee requires provider reservations or a dedicated channel allocation.

Ordering stays unavailable until this tenant saves a maximum line quantity, item quantity, basket units, basket lines, item subtotal, and payable amount. Those caps are positive integers and cannot exceed the commerce quantity and minor-unit ceilings. Monetary caps use this policy's currency and exponent. The payable cap is checked again after fees and taxes, once fulfillment is known.
